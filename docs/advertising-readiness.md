# Advertising layout and activation

The current visible placement is a labeled **house advertisement** linking to Toolroom's actual guides. It is independent of upload/download controls, has no third-party creative, cookie, impression tracking or Google ad script, and does not imply AdSense approval. The mobile dock stays unobstructed. Original content/navigation/privacy pages remain accessible.

Live Google advertising is not activated. Required operator configuration: approved publisher/site and real public origin; actual publisher/ad-unit IDs; consent/privacy messaging and appropriate reviewed CMP; exact CSP domains/loader behavior; publisher-specific ads.txt record if requested by Google; responsive filled/unfilled-ad and layout-shift testing. Do not invent IDs, seller records or an approval guarantee. Do not ask visitors to click ads, imitate download buttons or overlay ads on tool controls. Legal/operator identity and deployment gates in progress.md remain.

Sources reviewed: [Ad placement](https://support.google.com/adsense/answer/1346295?hl=en), [program policies](https://support.google.com/adsense/answer/48182?hl=en), [consent management](https://support.google.com/adsense/answer/7670013?hl=en). Google reviews eligibility; layout alone cannot assure acceptance.
